# Cover letter

Editor

*Journal of Mathematical Physics*

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Symbolic pure-state preimages for tabulated vertex spectra of fermionic one-body marginals through ten orbitals” as a Research Article in the *Journal of Mathematical Physics*.

The paper concerns the pure-state one-body fermionic marginal problem. It gives symbolic pure-state preimages for two vertices that Altunbulak and Klyachko checked only numerically in their four-fermion, nine-orbital analysis. One state is root-distinct. The second has two coherent exchange channels, and a finite rational certificate proves that its spectrum has no root-distinct preimage in any orthonormal orbital basis. These results, together with an exact reconstruction of all 103 vertices and a row-by-row concordance with the published inequalities, complete that moment-polytope description.

The manuscript also provides symbolic preimages for all 799 vertices of nine tabulated rational H-descriptions through ten orbitals. It distinguishes direct state identities, finite computer-assisted proofs, solver classifications, and the validity hypothesis retained in the ten-orbital corollary. An accompanying supplementary PDF describes the proof objects and their verification. A machine-readable research-data archive supplies the state formulas, rational H- and V-tables, certificates, source concordance, and checking programs; versioned project materials are additionally available through Zenodo.

The work fits the journal through its combination of quantum marginal geometry, exterior-power representation theory, exact many-body constructions, and finite mathematical computation. It complements general algorithms for computing moment polytopes by addressing the preimage problem for known rational descriptions.

This manuscript was previously submitted to *Journal of Physics A* and declined without external peer review; it has since been substantially revised. It is not under consideration elsewhere.

The author has no conflicts of interest.

Thank you for considering this work.
