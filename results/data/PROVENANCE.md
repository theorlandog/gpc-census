## holonomy_two_elementary.json (2-elementarity reduction and Theorem A')

Generated by `scripts/holonomy_two_elementary.py` from
`results/data/holonomy_fields.json`, guarded by
`tests/test_holonomy_two_elementary.py`, documented in
`docs/holonomy_two_elementary.md`. It reduces 2-elementarity of the holonomy
field to two conditions on `cos^2 Phi`: R1, that `deg_Q(cos^2 Phi) <= 2`; and
R2, that for `deg_Q(cos Phi) = 4` the norm `N(cos^2 Phi)` is a square in Q.

consequence at one-hop classes of size at most two, where Theorem A's formula
makes `cos Phi = n / sqrt(M)` with `n, M` rational, so `cos^2 Phi` is rational
and R1 holds at degree one.

## interference_certificates.json (EXACT global no-design certificates)

Generated by `scripts/generate_interference_certificates.py` for every negative
case in Proposition 1 and for v_B in Theorem 2. For an augmented determinant
incidence column a_T and target b = (lambda, 1), each primitive integer vector
y with y dot b > 0 defines the exact hitting clause

    P_y = {T : y dot a_T > 0}.

Every nonnegative incidence realization must use a determinant in P_y. The
artifact closes these clauses under permutations inside equal-spectrum blocks
and supplies a finite branch DAG proving that no one-hop-free support hits all
clauses. Totals: 12 records, 192 primitive integer vectors, 5141 expanded
clauses, and 226 proof nodes at maximum depth 8.

`scripts/verify_interference_certificates_standalone.py` uses only the Python
standard library and shares no code with the generator. It reconstructs the
incidence system, exact rational pairings, symmetry action, one-hop conflict
graph, and every branch. Solver runs remain discovery and cross-check evidence
only; the verifier does not invoke a solver.

Which vectors enter the artifact depends on the floating HiGHS solutions the
generator discovers, so `generate_interference_certificates.py --check` is a
same-toolchain reproducibility check, not a portable one. The artifact's
validity does not depend on it: the test suite runs the verifier, never the
generator, and any exact certificate set that verifies proves the same claim.

states_natural_orbital.jsonl ledger. It proves

    tr(Gamma_2^2) = 1438/529,
    tr(Gamma_2^3) = 6(2899-8t)/12167.

The cubic moment recovers the family parameter under the full one-body group
and separates the two Q(sqrt(35)) Galois walls. At t=0, exact spectral

## shared_loop_rationality.json (Theorem D and Theorem C')

## symplectic_invariants.json (lattice cones and exact stabilizers, all 799 rows)

EXACT. Generated by `scripts/symplectic_invariant_census.py`, pre-registered in
`docs/prereg_symplectic_invariants.md`, documented in
`docs/symplectic_invariants.md`, guarded by
`tests/test_symplectic_invariants.py`, and replayed with no project import by
`scripts/verify_symplectic_invariants_standalone.py`.

Two presentations of each census object, computed independently of the
combinatorial one already shipped.

LATTICE. The tangent cone at each vertex, built from the edge directions of the
polytope at that vertex. Edges are identified combinatorially from the V- and
H-representations: `[v, w]` is an edge exactly when the vertices saturating
every facet through both are exactly `v` and `w`. Each ray is reduced to its
primitive generator in the saturated lattice `Z^d` intersected with the
direction space of the affine hull, and the ray matrix against a basis of that
lattice gives the Smith invariant factors, the lattice index, and
unimodularity. The lattice basis is obtained by column-style Hermite reduction,
so it is saturated by construction rather than by a denominator-clearing
heuristic.

SYMPLECTIC. The exact projective stabilizer `Stab_{U(d)}([psi])` of the
certified state, its orbit dimension, the commutant of the state's own 1-RDM,
and the dimension of the commutant orbit through the state. Ranks are exact
over the algebraic number field the amplitudes generate, never numerical.

FIBER QUALIFICATION. Every stabilizer number is UNREDUCED and FIXED-RHO. No
first-order fiber tangent dimension is reported: `ker d(mu)` has dimension at
least `2*binom(d,n) - d^2` for trivial reasons at these ranks, so such a number
is a vacuous upper bound, and the prereg says so before the run rather than
after.

CONVENTION TRAP, found by the artifact's own internal check and pinned by a
test. Differentiating `rho_pq = <psi|a_p^* a_q|psi>` along the flow of
`X in u(d)` gives `d(rho) = [rho, X^T]`, so the subalgebra preserving `rho`,
which must contain the whole projective stabilizer, is the commutant of `rho^T`
and not of `rho`. The two agree identically for the 645 states whose 1-RDM is
real diagonal, and differ as subspaces for the other 154. Using `rho` produced
a commutant that failed to contain the stabilizer at `(3,10)#73`
(`stab_dim = 6` against `commutant_stab_dim = 4`), which is impossible, and
that single row is what exposed the error. The forced identity
`stab_dim == commutant_stab_dim` is now checked on every row and asserted by
the guard test, and `commutant_dim` is separately checked against the sum of
squared eigenvalue multiplicities of the vertex spectrum, which is an
independent confirmation that each certified state's 1-RDM really carries the
census spectrum.

## quantization_multiplicities.json (the representation-theoretic presentation)

EXACT. Generated by `scripts/quantization_multiplicity.py`, same
pre-registration, same guard test, same standalone replay.

For a vertex `lambda` with denominator `q` and `k` a multiple of `q`, the
multiplicity of the irreducible `U(d)` representation of highest weight
`k*lambda` in `Sym^k(wedge^n C^d)` is computed by the alternating Weyl formula
over exact integer weight counts, where the weight multiplicity is the number
of multisets of `k` occupation vectors with a given sum. Both halves are
integer counts, so the multiplicity is exact.

The method is validated on decompositions that can be checked by hand:
`Sym^1(wedge^n C^d)` returns multiplicity one at the fundamental weight and
zero elsewhere, and `Sym^2(wedge^2 C^4)` returns exactly
`V_(2,2,0,0) + V_(1,1,1,1)`. The standalone verifier additionally re-derives
every multiplicity with a dictionary generating-function DP instead of the
memoized suffix search, and checks the Weyl dimension identity
`sum_mu m(mu) dim V_mu = dim Sym^k(wedge^n C^d)` at several `(n, d, k)`.

Coverage is `(3,6)` and `(3,7)` under a wall-clock budget, not the whole
census: the weight count grows quickly in `k` and `d`. The `k` values actually
reached are recorded per vertex in `k_tested`, and vertices truncated by the
budget are named in the scorecard rather than silently dropped.

## quantization_characters.json (the period is a finite isotropy character)

EXACT. Generated by `scripts/quantization_character.py`, pre-registered in
`docs/prereg_quantization_character.md`, documented in
`docs/quantization_character.md`, guarded by
`tests/test_quantization_character.py`, and replayed with no project import by
`scripts/verify_quantization_character_standalone.py`.

Explains `results/data/quantization_multiplicities.json`. For each of the 14
`(3,6)` and `(3,7)` vertices, enumerates the `lambda`-preserving permutations
fixing the certified attainer's line, `wedge^n(P_sigma) psi = c psi`, and
computes the least `k` divisible by the spectrum denominator `q` with

    c^k * prod_i det(sigma|block_i)^(-k * lambda_i) = 1.

The lcm over those elements is the predicted period. Result: **14 of 14 exact
matches** against the measured periods, including all four period-doubling
vertices, each explained by a single explicit permutation.

The searched group is a SUBGROUP of the full stabilizer (permutations only, no
phased or non-monomial elements), so the predicted period can only ever divide
the measured one. Equality is therefore the falsifiable content and
divisibility is a correctness gate, which is how the pre-registration labels
them.

CONVENTION TRAP, found by that gate and pinned by a regression test. The
exponent on Levi block `i` is `k*lambda_i`, and the first version read it from
`integer_form[i]`, indexing an orbital-indexed array by a block counter. The
two agree only when every block is a singleton. The bug manufactured
obstructions at `(3,6)#0`, `(3,7)#0` and `(3,7)#1`, where the predicted period
came out strictly larger than the measured one, which the derivation forbids.
It left the four genuinely doubled vertices correct, so the equality prediction
alone would not have caught it.

The standalone replay recomputes the wedge action through `n x n` MINORS of the
permutation matrix, `(wedge^n h)_{T,S} = det(h[T,S])`, rather than through the
generator's combinatorial sorting sign, and reproduces every predicted period
and every element count with zero failures.

Not proved here: that a degree-`k` semi-invariant of weight `k*lambda` fails to
vanish at the certified attainer whenever the multiplicity is nonzero. That
hypothesis is stated in the pre-registration and its consequence is what the
artifact tests.
