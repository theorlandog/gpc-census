## Result

NO-GO, with witnesses rather than with a budget. The highest-weight semigroup
of `wedge^3 C^d` has a small, finite, degree-bounded generating set at `d = 6`
and at NO larger rank in scope. In the common window `m <= 11` the minimal
generator count runs 5, 29, 160, 340, 605 at `d = 6, 7, 8, 9, 10`, the
primitive part (generators a lower rank cannot supply by padding) runs 3, 24,
131, 180, 265, and from `d = 8` on the number of NEW generators is still
strictly rising at the last degree the window reaches, 36, 85 and 175
respectively. `(3,6)` is the only system whose generators stop: max degree 4,
and its held-out degree test PASSES. Every other system fails held-out
generation, in degree and in rank, and no tested valuation repairs it.

Read through Agent B's specialization lemma the statement sharpens. Those are
exact counts of minimal generators OF THE STABLE MONOID `Sigma_3^stab` under
its length filtration, so any generating set of `Sigma_3^stab` has at least 605
elements of degree `<= 11`, and a uniform bound on these counts is EQUIVALENT
to finite generation of the stable monoid. The measured growth is therefore a
direct attack on that finite generation, and it is exactly the "no uniformity
in `d`" gap that Agent B's dependency table records against every theorem in
the intended toolkit.

## The algebra and semigroup tested

ONE target, the preferred one in the brief. With `V = wedge^n C^d` and `U` the
upper unitriangular subgroup of `GL(d)`, the algebra is the HIGHEST-WEIGHT
ALGEBRA `A(n,d) = Sym(V)^U`, bigraded by symmetric-power degree `m` and by
`GL(d)` weight, and the semigroup is its support

    S(n,d) = { (m, lambda) : mult(V_lambda, Sym^m(wedge^n C^d)) > 0 }.

This is the object whose normalized support recovers the fermionic moment cone:
`closure { lambda/m : (m,lambda) in S(n,d) } = P(n,d)`, which is the same
identity `src/gpc_census/plethysm.py` and `scripts/plethysm_inner_hull.py`
already use to certify inner points of the polytope. The brief's combinatorial
FALLBACK target, the semigroup of certified facet-normal templates under
transport, is NOT computed here and no statement below mixes the two.

NO LONGER PROVISIONAL. This campaign began with a provisional definition
because the brief requires Agent B's to be read before any global claim. Agent
B's work has since merged (`docs/infinite_wedge_tca_bridge.md`), and the two
definitions AGREE, on the nose and including the convention: `Sigma_{N,d}` there
is `S(n,d)` here with the pair written in the other order, with `lambda` a
partition taken with no dual, no transpose and no complement, normalized as
`lambda/k`. Agent B pins that convention against two independently implemented
multiplicity routes at 93 of 93 pairs, with the control that substituting the
conjugate or the reversed complement changes the answer at 77 of them. Nothing
below is provisional and no claim here rests on a convention this project has
not fixed.

Agent B additionally PROVES the SPECIALIZATION LEMMA: with

    Sigma_N^stab = { (lambda, k) : <s_lambda, h_k[e_N]> > 0 }

the single `d`-independent monoid, `Sigma_{N,d} = Sigma_N^stab intersect
{ell(lambda) <= d}`. That upgrades this campaign's padding statement from a
bookkeeping gate to a corollary of a proved lemma, and it upgrades what the
generator counts below are ABOUT.

**Corollary (proved here, from Agent B's lemma).** The minimal generators of
`Sigma_N^stab` with `ell(lambda) <= d` are EXACTLY the minimal generators of
`Sigma_{N,d}`. Proof: an element `x` with `ell <= d` lies in both monoids. If
`x = a + b` in `Sigma_N^stab` with `a, b` nonzero then, parts being
nonnegative and `lambda_x = lambda_a + lambda_b`, both `ell(a)` and `ell(b)`
are at most `ell(x) <= d`, so the decomposition already lives in
`Sigma_{N,d}`; conversely a decomposition in `Sigma_{N,d}` is one in
`Sigma_N^stab`. So indecomposability agrees on `{ell <= d}`. QED

The counts in this note are therefore not merely finite-rank shadows. They are
exact counts of minimal generators OF THE STABLE MONOID under its length
filtration, which is precisely the uniformity Agent B's dependency table
records as missing from every theorem in the toolkit ("no uniformity in `d`",
against Hadziev-Grosshans and against Vergne-Walter and Bulois-Denis-Ressayre
alike).

The same corollary gives the sharp negative consequence. If `Sigma_N^stab` were
finitely generated by a set `G`, then every `Sigma_{N,d}` would be generated by
`G intersect {ell <= d} subset G`, so the minimal generator count of
`Sigma_{N,d}` would be bounded by `|G|` UNIFORMLY IN `d`. The measured counts
below are therefore a direct lower bound on `|G|`, and their growth is a direct
attack on finite generation of the stable monoid.

Two facts are used and are proved rather than assumed.

**`S(n,d)` is a semigroup.** `Sym(V)` is a domain, so its subalgebra `A(n,d)`
is a domain; `A(n,d)` is graded by `Z_{>=0} x Z^d` and the product of two
nonzero homogeneous elements is nonzero and homogeneous of the sum degree, so
the support is closed under addition. The run verifies this on every level of
every system as a gate, because a missing multiplicity shows up immediately as
a sum of two present values that is absent.

**`S(n,d)` is finitely generated.** `A(n,d)` is finitely generated by
Hadziev-Grosshans, so a finite minimal generating set exists at every `(n,d)`.
The campaign therefore measures the SIZE of that set and its growth, not its
existence.

## The three valuations

All three are valuations, and the one-line reason is given for each rather than
asserted.

| Tag | Valuation | Values in | Why it is a valuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `v2` | highest weight: lex-largest nonzero bidegree | `Z_{>=0} x Z^d` | top-degree map of a domain graded by a totally ordered group |
| `v1` | graded lex on Pluecker monomials, variables in lex order on the subset | `Z_{>=0}^{binom(d,n)}` | leading exponent for a term order, restricted to a domain subalgebra |
| `v3` | graded reverse lex on Pluecker monomials, variables in colex order on the subset | `Z_{>=0}^{binom(d,n)}` | same |

`v1` and `v3` both refine `v2`: every monomial of a weight-homogeneous element
carries the same weight, so the leading exponent determines `(m, lambda)`. The
fiber of the resulting surjection over `(m,lambda)` has size exactly
`mult(V_lambda, Sym^m V)`, which is the multiplicity information `v2` loses.
Both variable orders are stable under the rank embedding `C^d subset C^{d+1}`,
lex because it restricts to lex on subsets of `[d]` and colex because subsets
of `[d]` are an initial segment of colex on subsets of `[d+1]`.

GELFAND-TSETLIN WAS NOT COMPUTED, and the obstruction is exact rather than
budgetary. A GT or string valuation is indexed by GT patterns of a single
highest weight and lives on an irreducible module or on a flag variety.
`A(n,d)` is not such a module: its `(m,lambda)` piece is a MULTIPLICITY space,
carrying the trivial `GL(d)` action, so GT patterns do not index a basis of it.
The honest GT construction values the full ring `C[wedge^n C^d]` along a
complete flag in `G/B`, which is a rank `d(d-1)/2` valuation, 15 already at
`d = 6`, computed as an order of vanishing along a 15-step flag through a
Borel-generic point. That is outside the declared budget and no cheaper exact
implementation exists in this repository or in a permitted dependency. `v1` and
`v3` are the substitutes the brief allows. No statistic that is not a valuation
is called one anywhere in this campaign.

## The transports (Task 3)

**Padding, and it is exact.** `Gen(3,d) intersect {lambda_d = 0} = Gen(3,d-1)`
holds at all four rank steps, at 5, 29, 160 and 340 inherited generators. This
is a theorem twice over: elementarily, because coordinates are nonnegative so a
sum with last coordinate zero has both summands with last coordinate zero; and
as a corollary of Agent B's specialization lemma. It is also forced by the
implementation, since the multiplicity of `s_lambda` in `h_m[e_n]` does not
mention `d` once `ell(lambda) <= d`. So P3 is a BOOKKEEPING GATE and is not
offered as evidence for anything.

**Particle-hole, and it is a real test.** `lambda -> (m - lambda_d, ..., m -
lambda_1)` is a self-map exactly when `2n = d`, because `wedge^n C^d` is its own
dual twisted by `det` there and not otherwise. At the three self-dual systems
in scope it holds with zero failures on 951 values: `(3,6)` 189, `(4,8)` 446,
`(5,10)` 316. Unlike padding this is not forced by the code, since the two
sides are separate Murnaghan-Nakayama evaluations. The other five systems are
recorded NOT_APPLICABLE with the reason, because the complement lands in
`(d-n,d)`, a different algebra that is not in scope.

**Frozen-core lifting and ordered embeddings** are not separately tested here.
Agent B's campaign covers the embedding direction and refutes covariant
functoriality on facets while proving the contravariant restriction; nothing in
this campaign contradicts either, and the padding result above is the monoid
shadow of their restriction statement.

**After removing inherited elements**, the primitive generator counts are 3,
24, 131, 180 and 265. Padding therefore explains a shrinking share of the
generators as rank grows, not a growing one: the inherited fraction is 40
percent at `(3,7)` and 56 percent at `(3,10)`, but the absolute number of
generators that padding CANNOT supply grows monotonically at every step.

**VALUATION INCOMPATIBILITY WITH EMBEDDINGS. Excluded.** Padding is an exact
equality at every step, and both term orders were built to restrict correctly
along `C^d subset C^{d+1}`. The failure is not a convention bug, which is what
the abort criterion asked to be distinguished.

**MULTIPLICITY LOST BY THE VALUATION. Excluded as the cause.** `v2` does
collapse multiplicity spaces, and the run confirms it, with maximum
multiplicity reaching 47 at `(3,10)`. But `v1` and `v3` retain multiplicity
exactly, and on the matched window they produce the SAME or MORE generators, 5
and 6 against `v2`'s 5 at `(3,6)`, 14 and 15 against 14 at `(3,7)`, 15 and 15
against 15 at `(3,8)`. Refining the valuation cannot help, and not merely
empirically: refining can only split a value, hence can only enlarge the value
semigroup and weakly increase its minimal generating set.

**NON-FINITE GENERATION OF THE SAMPLED SEMIGROUP. Excluded at fixed `d`, and
now the live question at `d = infinity`.** Each `Sigma_{N,d}` IS finitely
generated by Hadziev-Grosshans. What the data attacks is finite generation of
the STABLE monoid: by the corollary above, a finite generating set `G` for
`Sigma_3^stab` would bound every rank's minimal generator count by `|G|`
uniformly, and the measured counts rise without flattening.

**COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET ONLY. Excluded.** The separation appears at the smallest
ranks in scope, where the computation is complete and cheap: `(3,6)` versus
`(3,7)` is already the difference between 5 generators stopping at degree 4 and
29 generators still arriving at degree 11. No larger budget can turn 29 back
into 5.

**AND A SEPARATE STRUCTURAL FINDING: THE SEMIGROUP IS NOT SATURATED, EVERYWHERE.**
The one model in this campaign that CAN over-predict is the saturated model,
which posits every lattice point of the cone. It over-predicts at all eight
systems, 5, 9, 14, 20, 25, 10, 16 and 7 spurious values. The smallest witness
is `(2, (1,1,1,1,1,1))` at `(3,6)`: `2 * lambda` lies in the semigroup at
degree 4 while `lambda` does not lie in it at degree 2. That is the Borland-
Dennis vertex, and it is the semigroup face of the quantization period 4 that
`docs/quantization_character.md` explains as an isotropy character. So any
compression scheme that models the semigroup by its cone is wrong before rank
enters the picture, and P9 confirms the connection quantitatively: the realized
multiples match the independently measured quantization period at 14 of 14
vertices.

## What is NOT proved

NOT PROVED, and none of it is a budget question dressed as a result.

- No statement about all `d`. Everything here is a finite-rank measurement
  inside a declared degree window. A finite-rank pattern is not a theorem about
  the rank variable, and the abort criterion was written to stop exactly this
  substitution.
- No Khovanskii or SAGBI basis is claimed for `A(n,d)` at any `d`. What is
  computed is the minimal generating set of the value semigroup inside a
  window, which is a lower bound on the basis and says nothing about relations.
- Nothing is proved about the algebra's presentation, its relations, its
  Koszulness, or its degeneration to a toric variety.
- The Gelfand-Tsetlin valuation was not tested, so "no valuation compresses" is
  NOT the claim. The claim is that the three tested valuations do not, and that
  the highest-weight one fails for a structural reason that a refinement cannot
  repair, since refining can only enlarge the value semigroup.
- `P3` is a bookkeeping gate, not evidence. Padding equality holds because the
  multiplicity of `s_lambda` in `h_m[e_n]` does not depend on the number of
  variables once `len(lambda) <= d`, which is also how the code computes it. It
  catches a broken implementation and nothing else. `P4`, particle-hole, is a
  genuine test, because the two sides are separate Murnaghan-Nakayama
  evaluations.
- Nothing here bears on the constructor, on facet generation at `(3,11)` or
  `(3,12)`, or on any census verdict.
