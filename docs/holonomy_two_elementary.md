# Proof attack on 2-elementarity: the reduction to two conditions

Status: the REDUCTION is PROVED below, and Theorem A' (a strengthening of the
shipped Theorem A) is PROVED. The two conditions the reduction leaves are
CONFIRMED census-wide, 82 of 82 and 13 of 13, and remain unproved at class
sizes 3 and 4. The mechanism sketch at the end is SPECULATIVE and labelled
so.

Certifying artifacts: `scripts/holonomy_two_elementary.py`,
`results/data/holonomy_two_elementary.json`,
`tests/test_holonomy_two_elementary.py`. Source ledger:
`results/data/holonomy_fields.json`.

Read `docs/holonomy_rationality.md` first for Theorem A, Proposition B, and
the hypotheses (H1) rational weights, (H2) rational channel targets,
(H3) channel-generated loops.

## The gap this attacks

Proposition B delivers a TOWER of quadratic extensions. That gives 2-power
degree and constructibility, and it does NOT give a 2-elementary Galois
group: `sqrt(1 + sqrt 2)` has minimal polynomial `x^4 - 2x^2 - 1` with group
D4. So the census's 82 of 82 multiquadratic verifications, and in particular
13 quartics that are all V4 with no D4 or C4, are unexplained by the proof in
hand. The scope decision was to ship the theorem at class size <= 2 and quote
the instance-level certification beyond it.

This note does not close the gap. It moves it: instead of "why is a 2-tower
multiquadratic", the open question becomes two sharp arithmetic statements
about ONE quantity, the square of the cosine.

## Theorem A' (proved, strengthens Theorem A)

**Theorem A'.** Assume (H1) and (H2), and let (A, B) be a one-hop class of
size exactly 2. Then `cos^2 Phi_12` is RATIONAL. Consequently the minimal
polynomial of `cos Phi_12` is `x - n` or `x^2 - n^2/M`, in particular EVEN,
and the Galois group is C1 or C2.

*Proof.* Theorem A gives

    cos Phi_12 = (|rho_AB|^2 - p_{u1} p_{v1} - p_{u2} p_{v2})
                 / (2 s_1 s_2 sqrt(p_{u1} p_{v1} p_{u2} p_{v2})).

Under (H1) and (H2) the numerator is a rational number `n`, and the
denominator is `+-2 sqrt(M)` with `M = p_{u1} p_{v1} p_{u2} p_{v2}` rational
and positive. So `cos Phi_12 = n / (2 sqrt M)` up to sign and
`cos^2 Phi_12 = n^2 / (4M)` is rational. ∎

The shipped Theorem A concludes only "degree 1 or 2". The evenness is the
part that generalises, and it is what the reduction below runs on: the field
is generated by a HALF-ANGLE, because `cos^2` (equivalently `cos 2Phi`) is
one level down.

**Census test, 0 violations.** Every loop whose state has all one-hop classes
of size at most 2 has `cos^2` rational: 47 of 47. Every loop with `cos^2`
irrational sits in a state carrying a class of size 3, which matches the
already-certified prediction that a non-monomial radicand needs a class of
size >= 3.

## The reduction (proved)

Write the two conditions:

> **(R1)** `deg_Q(cos^2 Phi) <= 2`.
>
> **(R2)** when `deg_Q(cos Phi) = 4`, the norm `N(cos^2 Phi)` taken over the
> quadratic field `Q(cos^2 Phi)` is a square in `Q`.

**Reduction Theorem.** (R1) implies `deg_Q(cos Phi) <= 4`, and

- if `cos^2 Phi` is rational, the minimal polynomial of `cos Phi` is linear or
  `x^2 - r`, so the group is C1 or C2;
- if `deg cos^2 Phi = 2` and `cos Phi` lies in `Q(cos^2 Phi)`, the group is C2;
- if `deg cos^2 Phi = 2` and `deg cos Phi = 4`, the minimal polynomial is an
  irreducible EVEN quartic `x^4 + p x^2 + q` with roots `+-rho_1, +-rho_2` and
  `q = rho_1^2 rho_2^2 = N(cos^2 Phi)`. By the classical criterion for even
  quartics,

      Gal = V4  iff  q is a square in Q*,
      Gal = C4  iff  q (p^2 - 4q) is a square in Q*,
      Gal = D4  otherwise.

  So (R2) is EXACTLY the V4 condition.

Every branch is 2-elementary, so (R1) and (R2) together imply Holonomy
Rationality on the degrees that occur. ∎

Note what this buys. The conjecture asked about a tower whose height and
shape vary from loop to loop. The reduction replaces it with two questions
about a single algebraic number per loop, and both are decidable by exact
rational arithmetic. It also says precisely how the conjecture could fail: a
loop with `deg cos^2 = 2`, `deg cos = 4`, and `q` a non-square would be a D4
or C4 counterexample, and nothing in the proved material forbids it.

`results/data/holonomy_two_elementary.json`, all 82 loops, exact sympy
arithmetic on the shipped closed forms.

| (deg cos, deg cos^2) | loops |
|---|---|
| (1, 1) | 37 |
| (2, 1) | 30 |
| (2, 2) | 2 |
| (4, 2) | 13 |

- **(R1) holds on 82 of 82.** `deg cos^2` is never more than 2.
- **(R2) holds on 13 of 13** quartics; every `q` is a rational square.
- The predicted groups are C1 37, C2 32, V4 13, which MATCHES the certified
  `galois_group` field of `holonomy_fields.json` exactly, group for group.

That last line is the real check. The reduction is not merely consistent with
the certification: it DERIVES it, from two conditions on `cos^2` alone,
without any of the group computation the original scan used.

**A boundary case worth recording.** Two loops, (5,10) index 113 loop 1 and
index 261 loop 1, have `deg cos = 2` with `deg cos^2 = 2` as well. Their
minimal polynomials are NOT even, so the half-angle picture fails there: they
are the two loops where `cos Phi` generates the same quadratic field as
`cos^2 Phi` rather than a quadratic extension of it. 2-elementarity survives
trivially at degree 2, but any argument that proves evenness in general must
explain why these two are exempt, and "the min poly is always even" is
FALSE as stated.

## What would close it, and the speculative mechanism

(R1) is the substantive one. Proposition B does not give it: from

    cos Phi_12 = (-alpha gamma +- beta sqrt(D)) / (alpha^2 + beta^2),
    D = alpha^2 + beta^2 - gamma^2,

squaring leaves a cross term `-+ 2 alpha gamma beta sqrt(D)`, so `cos^2` is
generally degree 4 as well. Two structural facts are worth recording from the
same algebra, both derived here:

1. With `alpha = P + Q c`, `beta = -Q S`, `gamma = G_0 + R c` (writing
   `c = cos psi`, `S = sin psi`, and `P, Q, R` for the `2 s_a s_b r_a r_b`
   coefficients), the `c^2` terms CANCEL in `alpha^2 + beta^2`:

       alpha^2 + beta^2 = P^2 + Q^2 + 2 P Q c,

   which is linear in `c`. This is why the tower stops at height 2 rather
   than growing with the class size.
2. `beta` is the only place `sin psi` enters, and it enters linearly, so
   `cos Phi_12 = A + B sin(psi) sqrt(D)` with `A, B, D` in `Q(c)`. The
   Galois conjugate over `Q(c)` is obtained by `sin psi -> -sin psi`.

**Speculative mechanism (not proved, and the best candidate on the table).**
The conjugate in point 2 is `sin psi -> -sin psi`, which is exactly TIME
REVERSAL on the fiber: sending every phase `theta -> -theta` maps a fiber
point to a fiber point (the equations have real coefficients and `rho` is
real on these states), fixes every holonomy COSINE, and flips every sine.
Likewise the sign-flip freedom `c_t -> -c_t` that preserves the fiber shifts
a loop holonomy by `pi k_t` and so flips `cos Phi` when `k_t` is odd; the
v_B record already certifies a 2-torsion coset of such flips, 64 of 128 sign
patterns over one weight vector.

The conjecture would follow if Galois acted on the loop holonomies only
through these fiber symmetries, because time reversal and the sign flips are
INVOLUTIONS and they commute, so the group they generate is elementary
abelian and contains no element of order 4. D4 and C4 both need one. That is
the "conjugation-involution / Galois-commutant" idea stated precisely enough
to be attacked.

It is not proved, and the honest obstruction is stated: Galois permutes the
COMPLEX solutions of the fiber equations, while time reversal and sign flips
are symmetries of the REAL solution set. Nothing here shows the Galois orbit
of a physical fiber point stays physical. The v_B Galois-orbit result (orbit
size 2, both roots attain exactly) is the one census instance where that was
checked and it held; one instance is not a proof, and it is the same gap that
Rigidity-Rationality's Galois-descent step runs into.

The two loops with a non-even minimal polynomial are the sharpest available
test of the mechanism: whatever explains evenness must also explain them.
