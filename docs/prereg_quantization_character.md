## What is being tested

`docs/symplectic_invariants.md` recorded a new invariant and left its mechanism
open: the multiplicity of highest weight `k*lambda` in `Sym^k(wedge^n C^d)` is 1
on multiples of a PERIOD `p` and 0 elsewhere, with `p = q` at ten of the
fourteen `(3,6)` and `(3,7)` vertices and `p = 2q` at the other four, where `q`
is the spectrum denominator. The period-doubling was unexplained except at
`(3,6)#3`, where it matches the classical fact that `C[wedge^3 C^6]^{SL(6)}` is
generated by a quartic.

This campaign tests whether the period is exactly a finite isotropy character
of the certified attaining state.

## The mechanism, derived before the run

Let `L` be the Levi `GL(m_1) x ... x GL(m_r)` of blocks of equal `lambda`, so
`k*lambda` is a character of `L`:

    chi_{k lambda}(h) = prod_i det(h_i)^{k lambda_i},

which is a genuine character of `L` because `lambda` is constant on each block.
Let `psi` be the certified attainer and let `h` in `L` stabilize the LINE, so
`wedge^n(h) psi = c(h) psi` for a scalar `c(h)`.

Suppose `f` is a degree-`k` semi-invariant of weight `k*lambda` with
`f(psi) != 0`. Then

    f(wedge^n(h) psi) = f(c(h) psi) = c(h)^k f(psi),
    f(wedge^n(h) psi) = chi_{k lambda}(h) f(psi),

so `c(h)^k = prod_i det(h_i)^{k lambda_i}`. Define

    omega(h) = c(h) / prod_i det(h_i)^{lambda_i},

well defined projectively: rescaling `h -> z h` sends `c -> z^n c` and
`prod_i det(h_i)^{lambda_i} -> z^{sum_j lambda_j} prod_i det(h_i)^{lambda_i}`,
and `sum_j lambda_j = n`, so `omega` is unchanged. The obstruction is then

    m_k(lambda) != 0  ==>  q | k  and  omega(h)^k = 1 for every h.

So the admissible `k` are contained in the multiples of

    p_pred = lcm over h of ( least k with q | k and omega(h)^k = 1 ).

STATED HYPOTHESIS, not proved here: that a semi-invariant of weight `k*lambda`
exists which does not vanish at the certified attainer whenever `m_k != 0`.
This is the standard attainment statement for a vertex whose reduced space is a
point, and every nonzero multiplicity measured in the previous campaign is
exactly 1, which is consistent with it. The campaign tests the consequence, not
the hypothesis.

`omega` is trivial on the identity component. It is a continuous character of a
connected group into roots of unity wherever `m_k` is ever nonzero, hence
trivial there, so the whole obstruction lives on `pi_0` of the stabilizer.
That is why a finite search is the right search.

## What is searched, and what that costs

Only `lambda`-preserving PERMUTATION elements: `sigma` in `S_d` with
`lambda_{sigma(i)} = lambda_i`, acting by `wedge^n(P_sigma)`. This is a
subgroup of the full stabilizer, so the search can MISS obstructions carried by
phased or non-monomial components. It cannot invent them.

Consequence, and it is the asymmetry that makes the test meaningful:
`p_pred` always divides `p_measured`, forced by the derivation above. So
`p_pred | p_measured` is UNINFORMATIVE as a prediction and is recorded only as
a correctness gate on the code. The falsifiable content is equality.

## Disclosed pilot

The mechanism was checked by hand at `(3,6)#3` before this file was written,
and the hand derivation was then reproduced in a throwaway script on that one
vertex: `sigma = (0 3)(1 4)(2 5)` stabilizes `psi = (|012> + |345>)/sqrt(2)`
with `c = 1`, has block determinant `-1` on the single block, and gives
`omega^k = (-1)^{-k/2}`, trivial exactly when `4 | k`. Thirty-six
`lambda`-preserving permutations stabilize the line and the lcm of their least
admissible `k` is 4, the measured period. No other vertex was examined.

## Power caveat, stated before scoring

Equality at all 14 would show that permutation isotropy accounts for every
period on this population. It would NOT prove the hypothesis above, would not
show the permutation stabilizer is the whole stabilizer, and would not extend
to any vertex outside `(3,6)` and `(3,7)`. With only 3 independent
period-doubling instances, a PASS is suggestive, not decisive.

    image of `(0 3)(1 4)(2 5)`, fixing the empty orbital. Expectation: PASS,
