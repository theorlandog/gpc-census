# Census support geometry and observable-order campaign

## Campaign objective

Compute the support-polytope and phase-observability invariants of all 799
certified census states, then determine which invariant actually controls
exact reconstruction from the 2-RDM.

The central quantities are

\[
\dim P_S=\operatorname{rank}A_1-1,
\qquad
\kappa(S)=|S|-\operatorname{rank}A_1,
\]

and the collision-free coherence graphs \(G_q(S)\) at RDM orders
\(q=2,\ldots,N\).

## First exact census-wide conclusion

The already merged exact atlas states:

- 63 supports have positive singleton-incidence nullity;
- all 63 are INTERFERENCE records;
- all 156 INTERFERENCE records are 2-RDM certified;
- all 28 order-two exceptions are DESIGN-INT records.

Since

\[
\kappa(S)=|S|-\operatorname{rank}A_1
\]

is exactly singleton-incidence nullity, it follows immediately that

\[
\boxed{
\kappa(S)>0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
S\text{ is 2-RDM certified}
}
\]

for every support in the current census, while

\[
\boxed{
q_{\min}(S)>2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\kappa(S)=0.
}
\]

Thus intrinsic non-toric phase complexity is not the cause of order-two
failure. In this dataset, the difficult interference carriers are all visible
to the 2-RDM, including every positive-\(\kappa\) carrier.

The implication should be read as scoped to this census, which is how the
artifact names it. Positive \(\kappa\) first becomes possible at carrier size
six, and 26 of the 28 failures sit at sizes two through four where
\(\kappa=0\) is forced by the support size alone. Restricted to sizes six
through nine, where both classes occur, the comparison is 0 failures among 58
positive-\(\kappa\) carriers against 2 among 356 simplex carriers, which is
not by itself decisive. The safe statement is the negative one: intrinsic
phase complexity is not the obstruction.

The 28 exceptions are support simplices: all relative phases are generated by
orbital rephasings, but their determinant overlap graph does not transmit those
phases through two-body channels.

## Physics interpretation

There are two independent notions of many-body complexity.

### Interference complexity

\(\kappa(S)\) counts phase directions not generated by diagonal one-body
orbital rotations. Positive \(\kappa\) signals intrinsically correlated phase
structure.

### Observable transport range

The graph \(G_q(S)\) connects determinants whose coherence appears alone in a
\(q\)-RDM channel. Its connectivity asks whether an order-\(q\) observable can
transport a phase reference across the carrier.

The census shows these complexities are almost orthogonal:

- interference carriers can have large internal phase complexity yet remain
  completely observable at order two;
- simple toric carriers can fail order-two reconstruction because their
  determinants are too far apart in excitation distance.

This gives a new organizing principle:

> The RDM order needed for pure-state reconstruction is controlled primarily
> by the interaction range of the support hypergraph, not by the number of
> intrinsically many-body phase directions.

## Useful theorem for design carriers

For a one-hop-free design support, no determinant pair differs by one
excitation. A \(q\)-body operator can connect determinants \(D_a,D_b\) only
when

\[
|D_a\cap D_b|\ge N-q.
\]

Therefore a necessary condition for order-\(q\) reconstruction is connectivity
of the excitation-distance graph with edges

\[
\operatorname{dist}(D_a,D_b)=N-|D_a\cap D_b|\le q.
\]

When every such channel is collision-free, this condition is also sufficient
under full \(q\)-incidence rank.

For the 28 merged exceptions, the artifact reports no collided order-two
channels. Their failure is therefore a pure overlap-connectivity obstruction,
not destructive channel interference.

One reading note on the order field. The scan runs over \(q=2,\ldots,N\), so
`minimum_collision_free_reconstruction_order` is a minimum over \(q\ge2\).
Two determinants differing by a single excitation are already connected by a
one-body channel, and such carriers are reported as order two rather than
order one. Order two is the question the campaign exists to answer, but the
field name is not literally the global minimum.

The summary artifact explicitly checks:

- whether every positive-\(\kappa\) support is interference;
- whether every positive-\(\kappa\) support is order-two certified;
- whether every order-two exception has \(\kappa=0\).

## Next physics calculation

Use the 28 simplicial exceptions as exact models of **observable locality**.
For each support, construct the minimal \(q\)-body Hamiltonian whose support
graph becomes connected and compare it with the minimum reconstructing RDM
order. This tests the proposed dynamical law

\[
q_{\mathrm{control}}(S)=q_{\mathrm{observe}}(S),
\]

or at least whether both are determined by the same excitation-distance
filtration.

If true, the minimum RDM order would have a direct physical meaning: it would
be the minimum interaction locality required both to resolve and to coherently
control the entire carrier.
